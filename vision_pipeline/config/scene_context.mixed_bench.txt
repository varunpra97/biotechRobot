A laboratory / workshop bench photographed from all sides with a handheld RGB-D
camera. Two white calibration cubes with ArUco markers and a few flat printed
markers are calibration equipment, not scene objects.

The bench holds assorted objects: labware (racks and holders with tubes, small
centrifuges, trays, containers) and general items (bottles, boxes, boards, metal
parts). Many are assemblies of several separate, removable parts in contact --
tubes standing in a rack, a rotor inside a housing, items standing in a tray, a
lid or cap sitting on its container -- and parts of one assembly can be made of
different materials (plastics, metals, wood, ceramic). The simulation needs every
removable part as its own object with its own material and mass.
